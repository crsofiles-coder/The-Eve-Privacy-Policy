## 1. Introduction & Core Privacy Philosophy

Welcome to **The Eve Task Reminder** ("we," "our," or "us"). We believe that your deadlines, tasks, and productivity habits are strictly personal. 

The Eve Task Reminder is built with a **privacy-first, offline-first architecture**:
- **No Mandatory Account:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide your email address to use The Eve Task Reminder.
- **Local-Only Storage by Default:** Your tasks, subtasks, deadlines, and schedules reside locally on your physical device.
- **No Data Brokering:** We do not sell, rent, monetize, or trade your personal data or task details to third-party data brokers or advertisers.

---

## 2. Information We Collect and How We Use It

### A. Information Stored Locally on Your Device
The following information is created by you and stored **exclusively within your device's local storage** (`SharedPreferences`):
- **Task Titles and Descriptions:** Scope of work and details entered by you.
- **Timing & Estimates:** Deadlines, estimated durations, and computed Guilt-Free Buffers.
- **Subtasks & Micro-Steps:** Actionable checklists generated by AI or created manually.
- **User Preferences:** Theme mode (System, Dark, Light), alarm sound toggles, notification window hours (e.g., 9:00 AM – 10:00 PM), and onboarding status.

*We do not sync or upload this data to a central user database.*

### B. Information Processed via Network (AI Task Breakdown)
When you choose to tap **"Break Down Task with AI"** or **"Regenerate"**:
- **Data Transmitted:** The specific task title, optional description, and estimated duration are transmitted securely over encrypted HTTPS to our AI processing gateway.
- **Purpose:** To return structured, psychologically-informed micro-steps (including the 2-minute gateway step) to help you overcome starting inertia.
- **Data Handling:** This task text is processed ephemerally in real-time. It is **not** used to build user advertising profiles and is **not** sold to third parties. If you do not use the AI generation feature, your task text never leaves your device.

### C. Financial & In-App Purchase Data (Subscriptions / Pro Upgrades)
When you purchase a subscription or unlock optional premium tiers:
- **Payment Processing:** All transactions are processed directly by **Google Play Billing** (Android) or the **Apple App Store** (iOS). 
- **Payment Credentials:** The Eve Task Reminder never collects, views, or stores your credit card numbers, bank details, or billing addresses.
- **Subscription Management (RevenueCat):** We use RevenueCat to securely validate anonymized receipt tokens and manage subscription statuses (e.g., trial period, active subscriber, auto-renewal status). RevenueCat processes an anonymous device/app user identifier solely to unlock features you have purchased.

## 3. Device Permissions & Why They Are Needed

The Eve Task Reminder requests only the minimal permissions required to deliver its core deadline reminder features:

| Permission | Platform | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| **Post Notifications** | Android 13+ / iOS | Used exclusively to deliver scheduled escalating alarms and threshold alerts (e.g., when your Guilt-Free Buffer reaches zero). |
| **Audio / Media Playback** | Android / iOS | Used to play local alarm sound chimes and audio cues. We **never** access your microphone or record audio. |
| **Network Access (Internet)** | Android / iOS | Used solely for ephemeral AI task breakdown requests and subscription entitlement checks. |

## 5. Data Retention, Backup, and Deletion

- **You Own Your Data:** Because your tasks and settings are stored locally on your device, you maintain complete custody of your information.
- **Instant Deletion:** You can delete any individual task or micro-step at any time within the app.
- **Complete Erasure:** Clearing the app’s data/cache in your device settings or uninstalling The Eve Task Reminder permanently and irreversibly erases 100% of your stored tasks and settings from your device.
